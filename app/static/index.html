Questions

How does it avoid making things up?

Before answering, AnswerWeave searches your content using keyword and semantic search together. Facts about your business come only from the passages it finds, with the sources shown. If nothing relevant turns up, it says so, offers to connect a person, and logs the question as a knowledge gap for you to answer.

Can it show my products or property listings?

Yes. It reads product and listing details that most store and property sites publish (names, prices, photos, stock, bedrooms), or you can upload a catalog CSV. Visitors see matching items as cards, filtered by budget, location and what they asked for.

Does it stay up to date when my website changes?

Turn on daily or weekly re-crawls and it re-reads your site in the background. Only changed pages are re-learned, new pages are added, and pages you delete are removed. You can also re-crawl any time with one click.

Do I need an OpenAI or Gemini API key?

No, it works out of the box. If you prefer, add your own key for Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, SiliconFlow or OpenRouter in settings, and AnswerWeave falls back to another provider automatically if one has an outage.

Can my team use it together?

Yes. Invite people by email as owners, admins or agents. Agents work the live inbox, leads and knowledge gaps; admins manage content and settings. Agencies can keep a separate workspace for each client.

Will the widget clash with my theme?

No. The widget renders inside a Shadow DOM, so your site's CSS doesn't leak in and its styles don't leak out. Match your brand with colours, avatar, fonts and custom CSS.

How does live takeover work?

When a visitor asks for a person, your team gets an alert. Open the conversation in the inbox and click Take over: automatic replies pause, the visitor sees that a person joined, and you chat directly. Release it when you're done.

Does it support Arabic and right-to-left languages?

Yes. It replies in the visitor's language, and when the visitor's browser is set to Arabic, or your assistant's greeting is in Arabic, the widget switches to a right-to-left layout automatically.
