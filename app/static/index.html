Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does AnswerWeave guarantee zero hallucinations?
Unlike generic bots that guess or invent answers, AnswerWeave uses strict extractive grounded RAG. If a question is not explicitly backed by your indexed documentation, the assistant politely declines and presents a lead capture form instead of fabricating facts.
How does Live Human Takeover work?
Your team can monitor live visitor sessions in the Admin Conversations Inbox. Clicking "Take Over" immediately halts automated AI replies, notifies the visitor that a human specialist has joined, and unlocks a live typing box for two-way chat. You can return the conversation to the AI at any time.
Will the widget break or clash with my WordPress theme?
Never. AnswerWeave renders entirely inside a browser Shadow DOM tree. Host WordPress, Elementor, Kadence, and Shopify stylesheets cannot penetrate or distort the widget components.
Do I need to pay for external OpenAI or Gemini API keys?
No. AnswerWeave includes an industrial-grade Zero-Key Local Extractive Engine that works 100% offline out-of-the-box. If you prefer to connect your own Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek, or OpenAI keys, you can do so in the settings with 1 click.
Does AnswerWeave support Arabic and RTL languages?
Yes! The widget automatically detects Arabic browser language (`ar`) or Arabic assistant text, flipping the entire layout to clean Right-to-Left (`dir="rtl"`) with mirrored icons and typography.
